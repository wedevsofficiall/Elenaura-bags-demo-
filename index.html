Our Story

Made Slow, Tied by Hand

Elenaura began with a single knot bag, hand-tied on a doorstep in Mumbai. No hardware, no machine seams — just fabric, folded and knotted into shape by hand.

Every piece is made in small batches by our studio team, using silk, velvet and raw-weave textiles. What you carry is a little imperfect, entirely handmade, and made to be yours alone.
